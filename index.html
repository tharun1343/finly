How a chit fund works
An agent sets up a ₹1,00,000 chit with 10 people (agent included). Everyone pays ₹10,000 per round. The agent takes round 1 — no auction.
From round 2, people who haven't taken yet bid. The highest bid wins — say Person B bids ₹10,000 and takes ₹90,000.

That ₹10,000 is shared by the 8 people still waiting: ₹1,250 each, so they pay ₹8,750 that round. B and the agent pay the full ₹10,000.
Not taken → what you pay in is savings, and every share you get is commission earned.
Taken → you owe the full amount every round until the chit ends — that's debt.

The last person left takes the pot in the final round with no auction.
